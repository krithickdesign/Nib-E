**Description:**
```
Nib-E is a free all-in-one PDF reader and PDF editor for Windows. Fill in and sign forms, highlight and annotate, merge, split and compress PDFs, redact for good and take notes beside your PDF. Every feature is free: no paywalls, no trial, no Pro plan, no watermark, no account.

WHY NIB-E
Tools that other PDF apps keep behind a subscription, like redaction, compressing, merging, form filling and signatures, are all included. Nothing is locked, and nothing is uploaded: Nib-E works on your PC, fully offline.

FILL AND SIGN
• Fill in any PDF form, even forms without form fields: Nib-E finds the boxes, lines and tick boxes and turns them into fields you can type in. Text matches the form's font.
• Sign PDFs: draw your signature once with a mouse, pen or finger, then place it on any line.

ANNOTATE AND WRITE
• Highlight, underline and strike through text, add sticky notes, shapes, arrows and text boxes.
• Stamps: a ready-made stamp library and a stamp designer for your own.
• Pen and highlighter with pen pressure and palm rejection. Saved as standard PDF comments.

STUDY MODE
• Your PDF beside a lined, grid or blank notebook. Write with a stylus, sketch or type. Notes are kept as you write and come back at the page where you stopped.

PAGES AND FILES
• Page manager: insert, rotate, reorder, delete and extract pages (split a PDF).
• Merge PDFs and images into one file.
• Compress PDFs: reduce the file size for e-mail and uploads.
• Convert: export pages as images, turn photos into a PDF.
• Several PDFs open at once in tabs. Save writes back to the file you opened, and the earlier version can be restored.

PROTECT
• PDF redaction that really removes: text under the box is deleted and picture pixels are erased, then Nib-E checks that nothing is left underneath.
• Password-protect PDFs with AES-256.
• Safe mode: scripts inside PDFs never run, and links or attachments can't open anything by themselves.

PRIVACY
No account, no ads, no tracking, no AI. Nib-E itself never connects to the internet; the Microsoft Store installs updates. Windows lists desktop apps like Nib-E as "Uses all system resources" because they are classic Windows programs; Nib-E only reads and writes the files you open or save.

Nib-E is free and open source (MIT licence).
```
